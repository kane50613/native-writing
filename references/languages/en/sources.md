# English punctuation: sources and limits

Accessed 2026-09-05. This is a qualitative sample, not a frequency study or an authorship test. See [earlier research](../../research/initial-samples.md) for the original blog, community, and product samples. The [punctuation guide](punctuation.md) combines scoped observations with editorial defaults; its exercises are original.

| Source | Access and context | Observation and use | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Binghamton University research summary](https://www.binghamton.edu/news/story/873/study-punctuation-in-text-messages-helps-replace-cues-found-in-face-to-face), 2017-11-14 | Original institutional article about experiments involving short text responses | A final period can affect interpretation in a short exchange. Consider tone before adding one to a casual reply. | This does not establish how all English speakers write, diagnose anger, or demonstrate the same effect in Chinese. |
| [Oxford University punctuation guide](https://www.ox.ac.uk/about/the-university/brand/style-guide/punctuation), revision date not established | Original house-style page, including quotation and list sections | Provides conventions for quotation nesting, logical punctuation placement, and list endings. Follow a chosen house style consistently. | University house style is not a universal British publishing rule. Its sentence-list endings differ from Microsoft's. |
| [Microsoft: periods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/punctuation/periods), updated 2026-07-06 | Original documentation style guide | Separates complete sentences from headings, labels, and short list items. Useful for distinguishing UI components from explanatory prose. | A current product-writing convention, not historical evidence about informal speech. |
| [Microsoft: commas](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/punctuation/commas), updated 2022-06-24; [dashes and hyphens](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/punctuation/dashes-hyphens/), updated 2025-04-29 | Original guide sections | Distinguishes clause structure, lists, compounds, ranges, and interruptions. Preserve the function rather than replacing every dash with a comma. | Serial commas and dash spacing remain house-style decisions. Literal code and names may need different characters. |
| [Microsoft: quotation marks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/punctuation/quotation-marks), updated 2024-10-17; [ellipses](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/punctuation/ellipses), updated 2022-06-24 | Original guide sections | Offers US-oriented quotation placement and distinguishes omission and conversational uses. | Do not alter a real interface label or quoted source merely to conform to prose typography. |
| [Chicago: ellipses in dialogue](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/qanda/data/faq/topics/Quotations/faq0077.html), cites the 2017 edition; page revision not established | Public original FAQ | Fictional pauses and trailing speech need different treatment from omissions in quoted evidence. | One publisher's convention. Preserve the scene's meaning and check the requested edition for production work. |
| [Lewis Carroll, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11/pg11-images.html), first published 1865 | cmux browser; opening of chapter I, approximately twelve paragraphs | Narrative uses periods, semicolons, parentheses, quoted speech, and dash asides for different relationships and rhythms. | Digital edition, not an original-print facsimile. Historical prose is evidence of available forms, not the default voice for contemporary writing. |
| [Julia Evans, Write about what you've struggled with](https://jvns.ca/blog/2021/05/24/blog-about-what-you-ve-struggled-with/), 2021-05-24 | Original blog text; revisited through web research, previously browsed for the initial sample | Developed web prose retains sentence endings, questions, enthusiastic exclamations, and asides. | One author's voice; no new punctuation counts or verification of an unchanged 2021 text. |

The evidence is strongest for distinguishing short responses, developed prose, and edited product text. It does not establish platform-wide rates on Reddit or Hacker News. A manuscript's publisher, a product's existing strings, and the writer's own consistent practice can override a working default.
