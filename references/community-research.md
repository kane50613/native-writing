# Writing research

Read this when researching a requested voice or reviewing the evidence behind this skill. These observations informed editorial choices; they are not a representative study of language communities.

## When gathering more samples

Choose public writing that matches the audience and purpose. Read enough context to understand what the passage is doing. Compare several unrelated writers and include blogs, documents, or actual product surfaces when those are the target. Prefer original-language articles published before 2022 for baseline prose. Check for later revisions and auto-translated editions: an old publication date alone does not establish the age or authorship of the text now displayed. Use current product pages for current labels and behavior, without treating them as verified human-written samples.

Prefer the original page. If it is unavailable, try the search engine's indexed text, an available public archive, or a public repost. Label each access method and judge only the passage actually visible. After the original and a reasonable public alternative fail, record the gap and use another source. A login wall is not evidence about writing style.

Separate the observation from the editorial choice: describe how a passage communicates, then decide whether that habit fits the requested output. Popularity, platform membership, and slang do not establish quality or human authorship. Borrow general techniques, not personal stories, distinctive phrases, or an invented local identity.

## Samples inspected on 2026-09-05

### Hacker News: explanation and disagreement

[Write simply discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=34606254), February 2023. Original discussion opened. Read the early exchanges, including dqv, qsort, daveslash, and dang; the thread includes a quoted generated summary, which was excluded as a positive example.

Observation: replies identify the specific proposition under dispute and develop a reason or example. They mix sentence lengths, contractions, technical vocabulary, and conversational asides. Several distinguish clarity from a restricted vocabulary. Editorial choice: preserve useful precision and connected reasoning; assess readability at the level of the thought.

### Reddit: ordinary advice

[How do YOU cook rice?](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cooking/comments/obqiwy), July 2021. Search-index text inspected; direct page fetch failed.

Observation: visible replies use concrete equipment comparisons, preferences, and small jokes tied to the subject. Editorial choice: keep details that explain a judgment, and preserve existing humor when it fits the task. This is a language sample, not cooking guidance.

[Treating documentation like a product](https://www.reddit.com/r/programming/comments/10qzx1y). Search-index text inspected; direct page fetch failed. Publication date not independently confirmed.

Observation: one visible reply explains its preference in terms of where the writer looks for help. Editorial choice: express a preference with its practical reason. Limited excerpt, not evidence about the entire discussion.

### Dcard: everyday narrative and a concrete question

[跟一直漲價的房東周旋讓我好累啊](https://www.dcard.tw/f/rent/p/252992868). Original page and substantial search-index text available; publication date not independently confirmed.

Observation: the writer connects a complaint to events, amounts, conversations, and a final question. Longer stretches remain understandable through chronology and explicit participants, although the post would benefit from editing. Editorial choice: preserve the concrete chain of events and the actual question; do not import every digression or formatting habit. Treat allegations as the writer's account, not verified facts.

[#工作心得 小小工程師三個月的心得](https://www.dcard.tw/f/job/p/252727135). Search-index passage inspected; direct page fetch failed.

Observation: a complaint about colleagues sits alongside appreciation of other colleagues. Colloquial particles and emoji accompany that mixed stance. Editorial choice: preserve ambivalence and degree of feeling. The surface markers belong only where the writer and setting warrant them.

## Product and documentation samples

- [PostgreSQL 13: Transactions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/13/tutorial-transactions.html), read in cmux. An older-release document, not a verified pre-2022 snapshot. An English prose semicolon joins a qualification to the explanation that follows; SQL semicolons are a separate category. The bank-transfer example develops the concept before the command sequence. This supports retaining explanatory depth and useful terminology.
- [HackMD: 筆記權限設定](https://hackmd.io/s/how-to-set-permissions-tw), read in cmux; page showed a 2025 edit. The document names controls, distinguishes ownership from permissions, and describes consequences. Some terminology and tone vary within the page, so it is evidence of a real help article, not a model to copy wholesale.
- [SHOPLINE: 缺貨時可接單](https://support.shoplineapp.com/hc/zh-tw/articles/900004394543), read through indexed official text; direct fetch failed. Provides setting location, conditions, and exceptions. The language lesson is to keep the condition next to the behavior, not to shorten away distinctions.
- [inline Taiwan](https://inline.app/zh/?language=zh-tw), read in cmux after using the language switch on the redirected English page. The page pairs restaurant situations with features and includes a form explaining that it serves restaurant operators. A semicolon occurs in the deposit-management copy. The page also contains broad promotional claims; they were not adopted as a universal style. The form was read, not submitted.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/), opened through the web tool. Feature sections combine product explanations, screenshots/demo content, and distinct navigation or conversion actions. Demo conversations are product demonstrations, not verified community writing.
- [Apple Taiwan file-system documentation](https://support.apple.com/zh-tw/guide/disk-utility/-dsku19ed921c/mac) and [China edition](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/guide/disk-utility/dsku19ed921c/mac), indexed official text compared for locale terminology. Their shared topic makes vocabulary differences easier to distinguish from differences in subject matter.

Current documentation and product samples inform form and locale, not claims about pre-AI authorship. Product behavior and pricing in those pages were not imported into the fictional editing examples.

## Simplified Chinese samples

[V2EX: 打算写写博客，前辈们有什么建议么？](https://v2ex.com/t/723733), opened and read in cmux. Original post dated 2020-11-10, replies extend to 2021. Read contrasting replies, including descriptions of time spent on setup and distinctions between notes and articles. Several use explicit conditions and practical reasons. Some use semicolons, including reply 48's sequence of benefits and a drawback. Advice quality varies; slang and encouragement are not general requirements.

[V2EX: 我的笔记系统](https://v2ex.com/t/643274), February 2020, indexed excerpts read. One reply explains why notification prompts might work better after an article than before it. Useful as a concrete suggestion with its rationale, not as a verified UX rule.

[阮一峰: 科技爱好者周刊，第 98 期](https://www.ruanyifeng.com/blog/2020/03/weekly-issue-98.html), dated 2020-03-13, opened in cmux. Read the opening discussion of expression and several short article descriptions. The page separates editorial, promotional, linked-summary, and comment material. A semicolon separates two reasons within one linked-article summary. Use the original author's exposition as a prose sample; exclude advertising and attributed third-party claims from general rules and do not endorse factual claims merely because they are clearly written.

Editorial choice: draft in natural mainland vocabulary, preserve the stated conditions and reasoning, and adapt form to the task. Simplified Chinese is not produced by applying the Taiwan reference and converting characters.

## Limits

This is a small, purposive sample, weighted toward technology writing. English samples do not establish a national variety. Dcard supplies Taiwan-oriented Mandarin examples without proving any individual's origin. V2EX is also not proof of any member's nationality. Instagram and Threads coverage is weaker than the blogs and directly opened forums. The language guides combine observations with editorial judgment; they do not measure how often a population uses a construction or certify that a passage was written without AI.
